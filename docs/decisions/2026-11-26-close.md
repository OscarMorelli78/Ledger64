# Ledger64 — Development close / 2026-11-26

## Approved product decisions

1. Identity: procurement intelligence agent for SMEs across sectors.
2. Business model: Open Core, retain the existing MIT core; commercial extensions may be separately licensed. No retroactive restriction of MIT code.
3. Learning: continuous, evidence-based learning with human review; no autonomous changes to financial rules.
4. Privacy: exclusively local processing; no external inference or document transmission. Optional local models require verified offline configuration and network isolation.
5. Market: SMEs across sectors, not a sector-specific ERP.
6. Payments: detect and prevent potential improper payments; never initiate or execute transfers.
7. Interface: selectable modern business and Commodore 64-inspired themes. This is an approved design requirement, not a claim that the modern theme has been implemented.
8. Release: retain the experimental branch until every release gate passes. No merge to Main or production release at this close.

## Product mission

Discover documented potential overcharges and savings opportunities that an SME may not be aware of; explain each finding with reproducible calculations and documentary evidence. Distinguish exposure, estimated savings, reviewed price reductions and confirmed paid losses. Never claim payment proof without appropriate evidence.
