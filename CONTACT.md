# Contact / Contacto

- [Commercial enquiry form](site/contact.html) (open from the hosted landing site or a local checkout).
- [Open a public commercial enquiry](https://github.com/OscarMorelli78/Ledger64/issues/new?template=commercial.yml) (requires GitHub login and enabled Issues).
- [Community issues](https://github.com/OscarMorelli78/Ledger64/issues).

**Public channel:** do not enter names, email addresses, phone numbers, tax IDs, invoices, credentials, client names, financial values or confidential details. The form creates a draft in GitHub; the visitor must review and submit it. No analytics, CRM, private inbox or automatic email delivery is configured. Request a private channel in the public issue before sharing sensitive information. A dedicated business mailbox may be configured later; no personal email is published.

**Español:** el formulario prepara una consulta pública sin datos personales ni empresariales. Revisa el borrador y envíalo en GitHub. Si necesitas tratar información confidencial, solicita un canal privado antes de enviarla.
